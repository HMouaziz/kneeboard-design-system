# Style: Cold War US Day

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

A daylight United States military style for early Cold War aircraft.

## Visual Character

```txt
USAF technical order + flight-line inspection card + cockpit data plate
```

This is an issued technical document adapted for rapid cockpit scanning, not a
modern interface with period colors.

## Palette

```txt
paper: cool grey-green government stock
primary ink: blue-black
secondary ink: graphite
form rules: graphite / olive-grey
section bands: faded equipment-grey with warm stencil lettering
origin colors: insignia blue / off-white / identification red
critical mark: red maintenance stamp or pencil
reference fill: desaturated zinc-chromate grey-green
```

Avoid yellow parchment, glossy metal effects, bright modern blue, repeated
floating cards, and decorative military camouflage.

USAF insignia blue is historically appropriate, but reserve it for the
star-and-bar and very small identity details. It must not become the dominant
page-header, section-band, or rule color.

## Document Grammar

- Treat the page as a punched, bound technical-order card. Use a visible left
  binding spine and restrained punched holes.
- Use a dark asymmetric aircraft title plate with a clipped or angled end.
- Mount the roundel in a separate equipment/data-panel area rather than as a
  bookplate or heraldic mark.
- Place the technical-order issue/status block at the far right.
- Use solid stencil bands for page purpose and section names.
- Use numbered equipment-card blocks rather than British ledger tabs.
- Render limits as data plates or tabular schedule inserts.
- Use stencil-like condensed sans headings and monospaced values.
- Critical actions may look stamped, circled, or underlined in red.
- Use a restrained post-1947 US star-and-bar identification mark.

The US structure must not use the British book-serif title, open ledger tabs,
double-underlined page purpose, or Air Ministry form symmetry. Origin styles
define different physical-document metaphors, not merely different insignia.

## Emphasis

```txt
normal: issued procedure or reference entry
muted: location, context, or explanation
critical: red-stamped action gate
caution: narrow striped margin marker
data plate: limits, settings, or mode schedule
```
