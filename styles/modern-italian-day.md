# Style: Modern Italian Day

## Character

```txt
Aeronautica Militare flight-department folio + Italian industrial graphic design
```

Use cool ivory or aluminium-grey stock rather than bright white or yellow
paper. The tricolore is a registration and indexing device, not a banner.

## Structure

- asymmetric aircraft header
- narrow vertical tricolore register
- offset folio labels and document codes
- light ruled panels with modest physical shadows
- circular Italian roundel used as a compact origin mark

## Typography

Use a strong condensed italic aircraft title, narrow industrial section titles,
and a neutral humanist sans-serif for procedure text. Avoid screen-like,
futuristic, or American technical-order typography.

## Semantics

- green: section registration and normal grouping
- red: critical action gates and warnings
- neutral grey: forms, rules, and secondary information
- tricolore: origin and page registration only
