# Style: Modern French Day

Version: 2.0

## Character

```txt
Armee de l'Air squadron sortie card + copied mission working sheet
```

The page is an issued working document, not a Dassault brochure. Use rough
grey-green stock, carbon blue-black ink, faded form rules, rubber stamps, and
small registration errors. Delta geometry may appear as a clipped datum or
field mark, but must not make the sheet resemble a corporate identity system.

## Structure

- compact squadron form header with aircraft, sortie, and sheet fields
- page purpose applied as an outlined or stamped title
- open ruled sections rather than floating filled cards
- typewritten values and stencil-like preprinted labels
- compact French roundel rendered like a faded inspection stamp
- diagonal tricolore tape or registration slash used once
- sparse grease-pencil marks for critical actions

## Typography

Use condensed industrial sans-serif for aircraft and page titles, monospaced
typewriter text for values and codes, and a sturdy utilitarian sans-serif for
procedures. Avoid polished geometric display typography.

## Semantics

- blue-black: primary document structure
- faded air-force blue: form labels and registration
- red: critical action gates and warnings
- graphite: secondary notes and field rules
- tricolore: applied identification mark only

## Wear

- texture must remain matte and low contrast
- rules may vary slightly in weight
- stamps may be rotated by less than two degrees
- never add fake tears, coffee rings, camouflage, or ornamental distress
