# Style: Modern French Night

Version: 2.0

## Character

```txt
dim squadron sortie card + low-glare cockpit utility lighting
```

Retain the rough working-form grammar and page order. Use deep olive-charcoal
stock, warm grey type, subdued air-force blue, and dusty red grease-pencil
marks. The night sheet must still look copied and handled, not digitally
inverted.

## Night Rules

- no pure black, white, or luminous cyan
- preserve faded form rules, stamp outlines, and tricolour identification
- mute the roundel while keeping each ring distinct
- critical rows use a red side marker and dark mineral-red field
- helper notes remain clearly legible at kneeboard scale
- day and night variants must have identical content and page breaks
