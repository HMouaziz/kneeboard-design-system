# Treatment: French Interceptor

- grey-green or cool grey-blue operational stock
- faded air-force blue and carbon blue-black ink
- compact French roundel as an inspection mark
- French cockpit abbreviations retained where operationally useful
- tricolore restricted to applied identification tape or a small register
- stencil and typewriter hierarchy rather than corporate aerospace branding

Recommended archetype: `squadron-sortie-card`.
