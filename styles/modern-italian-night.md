# Style: Modern Italian Night

## Character

```txt
dim flight-line folio + subdued red instrument lighting
```

Retain the day layout and tricolore registration, but reduce large bright
surfaces. Use charcoal paper, warm grey text, muted green, and restrained
instrument red.

## Night Rules

- no pure black or pure white
- preserve section outlines and folio tabs
- critical rows use a red side marker plus dark red-brown fill
- keep the roundel subdued but recognizable
- use the same page breaks, section order, and typography as day
