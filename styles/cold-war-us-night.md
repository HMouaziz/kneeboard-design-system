# Style: Cold War US Night

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Low-glare counterpart to `cold-war-us-day.md`.

## Visual Character

```txt
USAF technical order under dim red cockpit and amber utility lighting
```

## Palette

```txt
paper: deep olive-charcoal
primary ink: warm low-luminance ivory
secondary ink: muted khaki-grey
form rules: muted olive-grey
section bands: dark equipment-grey with warm ivory lettering
origin colors: dim insignia blue / ivory / oxide red
critical mark: dusty red-orange
reference fill: dark instrument-grey
```

Avoid pure black, pure white, luminous green, and simple color inversion.
Keep identical content, page order, spacing, and document grammar to day mode.
