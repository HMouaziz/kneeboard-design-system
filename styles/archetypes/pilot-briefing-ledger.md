# Archetype: Pilot Briefing Ledger

## Artifact

A pilot-facing notebook, briefing card, or operations-room ledger.

## Grammar

- book or clipboard framing
- strong page title with understated aircraft registration
- ruled notes and checklist lines
- paper tabs, margin marks, or clipped inserts
- reference tables that resemble briefing-room schedules

## Suitable Uses

Aircraft whose operational story emphasizes pilot notes, briefing rooms,
scrambles, navigation, and personal working documents.
