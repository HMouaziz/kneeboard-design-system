# Archetype: Display-Team Flight Folio

## Artifact

A professionally produced flight-department or demonstration-team handbook.

## Grammar

- clean asymmetric header
- precise registration and folio labels
- lightly raised or shadowed sections
- modern industrial typography
- restrained service colors

## Suitable Uses

Trainers, aerobatic aircraft, demonstration teams, and aircraft whose public or
instructional identity is stronger than their combat identity.
