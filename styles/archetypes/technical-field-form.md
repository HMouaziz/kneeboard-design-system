# Archetype: Technical Field Form

## Artifact

An issued technical card used around an operational aircraft.

## Grammar

- compact registration header
- open ruled sections rather than floating cards
- typed or stamped values
- inspection fields and document codes
- sparse maintenance-pencil emphasis

## Suitable Uses

WWII and early Cold War aircraft whose identity is mechanical, procedural, and
closely associated with engineering or field servicing.

## Avoid

Modern UI panels, glossy effects, decorative distress, and large flag fields.
