# Archetype: Squadron Sortie Card

## Artifact

A copied mission working sheet carried by an operational fighter pilot.

## Grammar

- aircraft identification rail or compact mission header
- single page-purpose title
- sortie, revision, status, and sheet fields
- open ruled procedure blocks
- grease-pencil action gates
- applied identification tape or inspection stamp

## Suitable Uses

Interceptors and multirole fighters where the kneeboard should feel operational,
temporary, and mission-focused rather than like a manufacturer publication.
